Avi is an Assistant Professor at [The Wistar Institute](https://wistar.org/)
in Philadelphia, where he leads the Srivastava Lab. He is a computational
biologist by training and by temperament, and his research sits at the
intersection of statistical machine learning, high-performance algorithms,
and modern functional genomics.

Across his career, Avi has built methods and tools that the single-cell
community now uses every day &mdash; from the
[salmon](https://salmon.readthedocs.io/) /
[alevin](https://salmon.readthedocs.io/en/latest/alevin.html) /
[alevin-fry](https://github.com/COMBINE-lab/alevin-fry) lineage of
quantification tools, to multimodal integration frameworks underlying
[Signac](https://stuartlab.org/signac/) and
[Seurat&nbsp;v5](https://satijalab.org/seurat/), to the
[scCUT&amp;Tag-pro](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-022-01250-0)
assay-and-analysis pair for single-cell chromatin states. His work has
appeared in *Nature Methods*, *Nature Biotechnology*, *Cell*, *Genome
Biology*, *Bioinformatics*, and *Nature Reviews Genetics*.

Before joining Wistar, Avi was a postdoctoral fellow with
[Rahul Satija](https://satijalab.org/) at the
[New York Genome Center](https://www.nygenome.org/) and the
[Department of Biology at NYU](https://as.nyu.edu/departments/biology.html),
where he focused on multimodal single-cell analysis. He earned his Ph.D.
in [Computer Science at Stony Brook University](https://www.cs.stonybrook.edu/),
advised by [Rob Patro](https://combine-lab.github.io/), where his thesis
work introduced several of the algorithms that became the salmon/alevin
ecosystem. He holds a B.Tech. in Computer Science from the College of
Engineering, Roorkee.
