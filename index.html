We decode the splicing code
that cancer breaks.

One isoform. One cell. One truth at a time.

We build isoform-resolved single-cell platforms, probabilistic models, and high-performance algorithms that turn long-read single-cell data into a quantitative picture of how the genome is read, spliced, and rewritten — at the level of individual transcript isoforms, in individual cells, across differentiation, immune response, and disease.

Research themes

Research themes

Five interlocking threads

From bench to bit: we measure at single-cell resolution, quantify with rigour, integrate across modalities, decode regulatory logic, and apply it to the cancers that splicing breaks.

01
Isoform-resolved single-cell

BenchDrop-seq — PIP-seq capture + Oxford Nanopore on any lab bench, revealing the 95% of isoform diversity invisible to standard assays.
02
Scalable quantification

Memory-frugal algorithms (salmon · alevin · alevin-fry · Bagpiper) that make atlas-scale single-cell and long-read analysis fast and reproducible.
03
Uncertainty-aware models

Bayesian and probabilistic methods that propagate multi-mapping ambiguity all the way through quantification, differential analysis, and inference.
04
Multimodal integration

Joint models for RNA, chromatin, histone marks, and surface proteins — one cell described by one coherent state rather than disagreeing tables.
05
Regulatory genomics

Chromatin dynamics and gene-regulatory networks: which regulators move first, which programs follow, where the system becomes brittle in disease.
